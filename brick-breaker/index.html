Privacy Policy – Brick Breaker (br.quebrablocos)

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Brick Breaker is a game developed by Yan Venera. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Data collection

The app does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal or usage data. It has no ads, no analytics, no accounts and does not access the internet. It requests no permissions.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress (unlocked levels, stars, best scores) and the sound setting are saved only on your device. They are deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data. If Android backup is enabled on your device, this progress may be included in your personal Google backup, which is controlled by you and Google, not by the developer.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page.

Contact

Questions: ynvenera@gmail.com
